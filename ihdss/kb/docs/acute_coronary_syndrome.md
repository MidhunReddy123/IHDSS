# Acute coronary syndrome (ACS)
Presentation: chest pressure or pain radiating to arm or jaw, diaphoresis, nausea, dyspnea, lasting >20 minutes. Risk factors: smoking, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, diabetes, age.
Urgent work-up: 12-lead ECG within 10 minutes, serial high-sensitivity troponin. Elevated troponin with ischemic symptoms indicates myocardial infarction. This is an emergency requiring immediate escalation.
Treatment pathway: aspirin, anticoagulation, reperfusion strategy per ECG (STEMI vs NSTEMI).
Source: ACC/AHA chest pain guideline (summary).
