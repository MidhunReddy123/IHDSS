# Type 2 diabetes mellitus
Diagnosis: fasting plasma glucose >=126 mg/dL, HbA1c >=6.5%, 2-hour OGTT >=200 mg/dL, or random glucose >=200 mg/dL with classic symptoms. Confirm with repeat testing if asymptomatic.
Classic symptoms: polyuria, polydipsia, fatigue, blurred vision, unintentional weight loss. Risk factors: age, obesity, family history, hypertension.
Next steps: repeat HbA1c, lipid panel, urine albumin-creatinine ratio, eye and foot examination. First-line therapy is lifestyle change plus metformin if renal function allows.
Source: ADA Standards of Care (summary).
