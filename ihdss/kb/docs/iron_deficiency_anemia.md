# Iron deficiency anemia
Suspect with fatigue, pallor, dizziness, brittle nails, pica (ice craving), and sources of blood loss such as heavy menstrual bleeding or GI bleeding.
Labs: low hemoglobin (<12 g/dL women, <13.5 men), microcytosis (MCV <80 fL), low ferritin (<15 ng/mL is highly specific).
Next steps: identify the cause of iron loss (menstrual history; GI evaluation in men and postmenopausal women), oral iron replacement, recheck hemoglobin in 4 weeks.
Source: AGA/WHO anemia guidance (summary).
