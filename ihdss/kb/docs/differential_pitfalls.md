# Differential diagnosis cautions
Chest pain with dyspnea: also consider pulmonary embolism, pneumonia, pericarditis, aortic dissection. Fatigue with weight gain and bradycardia suggests hypothyroidism; fatigue with weight loss and thirst suggests diabetes. Shortness of breath with edema suggests heart failure or renal disease. Always rule out life-threatening causes first.
