# Congestive heart failure
Symptoms: exertional dyspnea, orthopnea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, peripheral edema, rapid weight gain. Signs: raised JVP, crackles, hypoxemia.
Labs: elevated BNP (>100 pg/mL) or NT-proBNP; hyponatremia and renal impairment indicate severity.
Next steps: echocardiogram for ejection fraction, ECG, chest radiograph, review precipitating drugs. NSAIDs worsen fluid retention and should be avoided. Treatment: loop diuretic for congestion, ACE inhibitor/ARNI, beta blocker, MRA, SGLT2 inhibitor.
Source: ACC/AHA/HFSA heart failure guideline (summary).
