# Primary hypothyroidism
Symptoms: fatigue, weight gain, cold intolerance, constipation, dry skin, hair thinning, low mood, bradycardia.
Diagnosis: elevated TSH with low free T4 (overt). Raised TSH with normal free T4 is subclinical. Often raises LDL cholesterol.
Next steps: anti-TPO antibodies to look for Hashimoto thyroiditis, start levothyroxine, recheck TSH in 6-8 weeks.
Source: ATA hypothyroidism guideline (summary).
