# Community-acquired pneumonia
Suspect with fever, productive cough, pleuritic chest pain, dyspnea, tachycardia, tachypnea, hypoxemia (SpO2 <94%). Labs: raised WBC and CRP.
Confirm with chest radiograph showing new infiltrate. Assess severity with CURB-65 (confusion, urea, respiratory rate, blood pressure, age >=65). Consider sputum culture, blood cultures, urinary antigens in moderate or severe disease.
Treatment: empiric antibiotics guided by severity; oxygen if hypoxemic.
Source: IDSA/ATS CAP guideline (summary).
